Withdrawn Wrong in both halves, and wrong in opposite directions. "Their annotations … stay attributed" is false today and stays false: ADR-0073 §1 gives an Annotation no author, no name, no "declared by", not stored and not rendered. "audit history stays attributed" is false today because there is no audit history, and becomes true under Settled #8 — the Act holds the username as a captured value, so removal can neither orphan the history nor be blocked by it. The sentence also describes a removal that usually cannot happen. Under the Act corpus this dialog would read as variant A.

Chosen The second line is the whole point. The eleven created_by columns name no ON DELETE, so they restrict, and removeAccount catches the violation and refuses (cmd/web/settings.go:479-486). The operator types an exact username to confirm, so a silent dialog puts the refusal after the typing — the worst available ordering. Declined Honest about success, silent about the common outcome. On any instance older than a day, a working admin's removal is refused.
